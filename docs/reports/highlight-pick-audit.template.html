Judgment rules and limits

This audit reviews every selected dish using its exact saved menu entry, meal, station, description and neighboring items. It checks that the selected item belongs to that meal. It does not use calories as a main-dish proxy and does not infer dietary safety.

  • Correct main includes composed dishes, substantial breakfast dishes, named meal bars and entrée proteins. A generic burger or grilled chicken can be a valid main even when a more specific dish would be a better highlight.
  • Weak / unclear includes standalone breakfast meats, staple oatmeal chosen over composed dishes, duplicate components of a selected bar, and names the menu does not explain well enough. These are not counted as clear side mistakes.
  • Wrong: side means a clear standalone accompaniment, such as the plain grains or vegetables identified in the old October 2 picks.
  • Other mains worth considering are examples, not a complete list of every eligible main. A five-item highlights list is allowed to leave out good mains. The strongest omission here is Collins returning only three brunch picks when other credible mains remain.
  • The October 2 original had a four-pick limit; the refined version allows five. This affects both quantity and coverage. The refined comparison uses one fixed final six-hall run, not each hall's best result.
  • Today's October 4 menus and picks were captured for this audit with GPT-OSS 120B, prompt version 5 and low reasoning effort. One initial Hoch response failed item/meal validation and was rejected; its retry succeeded. All published selections passed membership validation. Nothing from this audit has been deployed to the production API.
  • Oldenborg had no published October 4 menu. September 30 has saved picks for four halls; Hoch and Collins had no saved ready selection. Those missing results are not scored as wrong picks.
  • These are assistant judgments, not an independent human-reviewed answer key. Unclear dishes need better descriptions or a dining-hall review. “No clear side errors” does not mean perfect selections.
